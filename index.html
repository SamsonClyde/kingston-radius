Kingston Radius

One place for live music in the Hudson Valley.

An independent calendar covering 100+ venues within 90 minutes of Kingston, NY. Updated daily. Nothing for sale.

How to use it
Distance

Kingston only, 30, 60, or 90 minutes out. Covers Woodstock, Hudson, Poughkeepsie, Peekskill, and beyond.

Month

Filter to a specific month. Buttons appear automatically based on what's listed.

Venue

Use the dropdown to see everything coming up at one spot. Click any venue name to visit their site, or the city to open directions.

Independent project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any venue listed. All links go directly to the source.
