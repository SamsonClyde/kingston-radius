Privacy Policy.

Last updated October 28, 2026. This is the policy for the secret /all version of the site.

THE SHORT VERSION:

This version of Kingston Radius is the free live music calendar plus an optional Fun layer behind the ★. There are no accounts and it never asks for your email or any personal details. The only thing you can choose to give it is a leaderboard name. This version does not load Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager or ads. It does use Google Fonts, a weather service when the Fun layer is on, and OpenStreetMap map tiles when you open the Map. The details are below.

WHAT GETS COLLECTED:
  • Server information. The site is hosted by Netlify, which, like any web host, receives standard connection details such as your IP address and browser type when a page loads.
  • Fonts. Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when a page opens.
  • No analytics, no ads. These pages run no visit counters and show no ads, so nothing here is tracked for advertising.
STORED IN YOUR BROWSER:

The site saves a few things in your own browser's storage (not on our servers, and we can't see them): your light or dark mode choice, which view (List, Feed, Wall or Map) you used last, the shows you save with the heart, the zip code you enter for My Radius (see below), a saved copy of the show listings so pages load faster, and, with the Fun layer on, your Stubs and daily progress, your best Snake, Pinball and Memory Match scores, Blip's mood, a random player ID used for the leaderboard, and a note of what you've already seen. Saved shows stay on your device; they are never sent to us. You can clear all of this at any time in your browser settings.

MY RADIUS (ZIP CODES):

If you use the My Radius feature and enter a zip code, your browser sends that zip code to a free third-party lookup service, Zippopotam.us, to find its map location. Distances are then estimated in your browser. We never receive or store your zip code.

THE MAP VIEW:

Only when you open the Map view (including the handheld GPS in the Fun layer), your browser loads the Leaflet map library from Cloudflare's cdnjs and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Like any website you visit, those services can see your IP address, browser details, and which part of the map you are viewing. We don't receive that information. They have their own policies: OpenStreetMap and Cloudflare. If you never open the map, none of this happens.

THE FUN LAYER (THE ★):

The Fun layer is off until you press the ★. When it is on, your browser asks a free weather service, Open-Meteo, for the forecast at fixed Kingston coordinates. It never asks for or sends your own location. Games, Ray, the animals, the daily food and drink and the 8-Ball all run in your browser using the show listings the page already loaded. Nothing you do in them is sent to us, except as described next.

THE LEADERBOARD:

Only if you choose to sign the leaderboard, your browser sends us the name you typed, your current score and a random player ID that it made up. The ID is stored only as a one-way scrambled code so it can't be traced back to a browser, and it is used just to keep one name per player and to mark your row as yours. The name and score are kept in a small data file in the site's code repository on GitHub, and the top 25 names and scores are shown to anyone who opens the board. Your IP address is used only for a few seconds, to stop rapid repeat submissions, and is not stored. Names can't be changed once signed; email us to have one removed.

LINKS TO OTHER SITES:

Listings link to venue websites, ticket pages, and Google Maps. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and Kingston Radius is not affiliated with or responsible for them. Show information is gathered from public venue websites, newsletters, and social posts.

WHAT WE DON'T DO:

We don't sell or rent personal information, we don't run user accounts, we don't track you across other sites, and the site is not directed at children under 13.

YOUR CHOICES:

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use the opt-out links above, or browse in a private window. The site still works if you do. If you have a question about information related to you, email us.

CHANGES:

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
